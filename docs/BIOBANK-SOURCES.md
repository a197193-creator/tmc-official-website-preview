## Source distinctions

- The biobank prose describes 38 −80°C freezers and 35 liquid-nitrogen tanks, while its equipment table lists 47 and 25. The preview reproduces the published table and avoids presenting either set as a reconciled current total. A biobank content owner should resolve this discrepancy before the official transfer.
- Publication citation, indexing and accepted/published status fields are source snapshots, not independently refreshed bibliometric claims. Source titles have no paper URLs; no DOI links are invented.
- The supplied design's sample metric, example papers, draft contact persona, DAC form and turnaround promise are replaced with sourced content and working published destinations.
