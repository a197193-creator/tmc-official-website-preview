The exact observed source, handbook PDF, form, and spreadsheet links are recorded in `data/participant-sources.json`. Clinical content is a concise orientation to UKM's published information; the page links the complete procedures. Kit charges, deposit arrangements, diagnostic thresholds, and medication instructions were not added to the new page.

The location page links UKM's published recruitment list without presenting it as a current appointment schedule. Screening results retain UKM's stated dispatch timeframe and a contact route for delivery questions. The preview stores no contact update submissions, questionnaires, identity numbers, or health results.

All page photographs are from the supplied TMC archives; see [image sources](IMAGE-SOURCES.md). The main site navigation now has a single Participants entry linked to this page, and the duplicate participant button in the home header is removed.
