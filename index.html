About the cohort

A long-term view of health in Malaysia

The project was initiated in 2004 and approved by the Cabinet in 2005. Its purpose is to support research into how genes, environment and lifestyle relate to disease.

The current UKM website holds the official project history, objectives, directory and governance information.
