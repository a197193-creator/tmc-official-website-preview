About the study

TMC studies how genetic, environmental and lifestyle factors affect health. Its long-term data and biobank support research into risk factors and early signs of disease. The study also contributes to the Asia Cohort Consortium.

Read the full introduction →View the infographics

Research objectives

  1. Study how genetic, environmental and lifestyle factors interact to influence disease.
  2. Use genomic and proteomic research to look for early markers of cancer and other diseases.
  3. Build a national resource of health data and biospecimens for scientific research.
  4. Advance life sciences and biotechnology through international research collaboration.

Common questions

What happens during a screening visit?

UKM describes a visit of approximately 3–4 hours: questionnaires, blood and urine tests, and biophysical measurements. The team contacts participants to arrange appointments.

Screening visit guide

How will I receive my screening results?

UKM describes printed results sent through Pos Malaysia to the registered address, with a stated dispatch timeframe of 14 working days from recruitment. Contact TMC about delivery questions.
